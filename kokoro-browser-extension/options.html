Kokoro backend

Narration uses the local Kokoro tray app, and falls back to the platform voice otherwise. Nothing is sent off this machine either way.

Pairing is required once, on every browser. The tray app answers on 127.0.0.1 and only to browsers holding its code, so this page is how you hand it over.
